## Introduction
As academic publishers adopt automated systems to assign manuscript peer reviewers, the underlying algorithms face increasing scrutiny. There is an active risk of path-dependency and echo-chambers where specific schools of thought dominate.

## Ethical Boundaries
We present a dual-layer auditing framework designed to detect citation feedback loops and geographic clustering. This serves as an initial blueprint for guaranteeing cognitive diversity in editor assignments.
