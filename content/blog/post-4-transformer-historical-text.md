## Introduction
Digitized archives are transforming historical scholarship, offering computational access to centuries of literature. However, Optical Character Recognition (OCR) errors introduce structural noise that traditional keyword matching fails to overcome.

## Methodology
We demonstrate how domain-refined transformers with self-attention layers can reconstruct fragmented phrases and map shifting semantic landscapes over multi-decade sequences.
