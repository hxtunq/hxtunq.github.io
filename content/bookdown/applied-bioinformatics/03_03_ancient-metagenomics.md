Ancient metagenomics deals with highly degraded, fragmented historical DNA extracted from archaeological specimens, subfossils, sedimentary cores, or museum collections.

### Specialized Considerations
- **Post-Mortem Damage (PMD):** Deamination of cytosine to uracil resulting in characteristic C-to-T transitions at 5' ends and G-to-A at 3' ends.
- **Short Fragment Lengths:** Sequences typically range between 30-70 bp.
- **Modern Contamination Filtering:** Strict authentication with damage calculation tools.
- **Toolkits:** mapDamage2, damageprofiler, eager (nf-core/eager), HOPS, MALT.
