Metabolomics is the comprehensive qualitative and quantitative analysis of all small molecules (metabolites) present in biological systems, biofluids, cells, and tissues.

### Key Aspects
- **Analytical Platforms:** Liquid Chromatography-Mass Spectrometry (LC-MS), Gas Chromatography-Mass Spectrometry (GC-MS), Nuclear Magnetic Resonance (NMR).
- **Untargeted vs. Targeted Metabolomics:** Comprehensive discovery vs. absolute quantification of predefined panels.
- **Data Processing:** Peak picking, retention time alignment (XCMS, MZmine).
- **Compound Identification:** Molecular networking (GNPS), spectral library matching (HMDB, METLIN).
