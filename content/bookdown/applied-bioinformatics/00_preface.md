Welcome to the **Applied Bioinformatics** resource repository. This documentation focuses on hands-on computational biology workflows, practical analysis pipelines, and applied data science strategies in biological research.

### Core Areas
- **Sequence Processing & Quality Control:** High-throughput FASTQ processing, trimming, and alignment.
- **Variant Calling & Annotation:** Germline and somatic variant discovery using GATK, BCFtools, and VEP.
- **Transcriptomics & Gene Expression:** Differential expression analysis (DESeq2, edgeR) and single-cell pipelines.
- **Microbial & Metagenomic Workflows:** Amplicon (16S rRNA/DADA2) and shotgun metagenomics profiling.
