Whole Exome Sequencing (WES) selectively targets and sequences the ~1-2% of the genome that codes for functional proteins (exons), where the majority of disease-causing mutations reside.

### Key Considerations
- **Target Capture Kits:** Agilent SureSelect, Illumina TruSeq, Twist Exome.
- **On-Target Coverage & Depth:** Target >50x–100x mean coverage for high sensitivity in clinical germline & somatic variant calling.
- **Bait & Capture Bias Handling:** Off-target read filtering and capture efficiency estimation.
