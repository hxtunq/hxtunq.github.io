Welcome to the **Research Skills** documentation repository. This section compiles guidelines, best practices, computational workflows, and critical methods for literature review, hypothesis generation, reproducible science, and paper drafting in biological & computational sciences.

### Core Pillars
- **Literature Review & Reference Management:** Finding, synthesizing, and organizing academic papers using Zotero, Connected Papers, and semantic search.
- **Scientific Communication:** Effective writing for research papers, preparing reproducible figures, and structured academic presentations.
- **Reproducible Computing:** Version control with Git/GitHub, containerization with Docker/Singularity, and workflow automation.
